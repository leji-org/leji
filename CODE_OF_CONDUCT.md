# Code of Conduct

Be direct about the work, and decent to the people.

**Expected**: technical arguments grounded in the spec, the code, or a reproducible case; good-faith review; assuming competence in others; welcoming first-time contributors and independent implementers.

**Not tolerated**: personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory remarks, sustained disruption, or publishing others' private information. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space representing the project.

**Enforcement**: the steward ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) moderates. Reports go to [hello@leji.org](mailto:hello@leji.org); reports about the steward go through GitHub's abuse reporting, which is the only channel independent of the steward while stewardship rests with one party. Consequences scale from a warning to removal from project spaces.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.
