leji freshness: Report review horizons across category documents and agent
   profiles.

Usage: leji freshness [--strict] [--root <dir>] [--json]

Lists documents whose freshness.reviewAfter horizon has passed (expired) or
falls within the next 30 days (upcoming). Report-only by default; expired
horizons are warnings.

Options:
   --strict            Treat expired horizons as errors instead of warnings.

Global options: see leji --help.

Examples:
   leji freshness
   leji freshness --strict --json

Full reference: https://leji.org/cli/
